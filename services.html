Janitorial & Custodial

Launch service

Recurring cleaning for offices, public buildings, ranger and field stations, dining facilities, and commercial spaces, scheduled to fit operating hours, including evenings and weekends.

Typical tasks

  • Restroom cleaning, disinfecting, and restocking
  • Trash and recycling removal
  • Vacuuming, sweeping, and mopping
  • Dusting and high-touch surface disinfecting
  • Break room and kitchen cleaning
  • Periodic carpet care, window cleaning, and deep cleans

Service area: Solano County, Northern California, and nearby regions.
