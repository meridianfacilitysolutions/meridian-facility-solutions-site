Why Meridian

Facility managers should not have to coordinate a different vendor for every task. Services are delivered by trained crews and vetted partners under our management and quality control.

How we work

  1. 1

    Scope

    We review the statement of work, walk the site where possible, and confirm tasks, frequencies, hours, and access rules in writing.

  2. 2

    Assign crews and partners

    We coordinate vetted subcontractors who perform the work under our management and quality control, and set the schedule, supplies, and site contacts.

  3. 3

    Quality control

    We inspect against the scope, track issues to closure, and report back, so you have one contact for every question.
