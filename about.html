About Meridian

A Fairfield, California company built on a simple idea: facility owners and managers deserve one accountable contact for the work done in their buildings.

Our story

I started Meridian because I watched too many businesses get bounced between vendors who didn't talk to each other. One company for the floors, another for the lights, a third for the landscaping, and nobody owning the result. I wanted to build something where a facility manager has a single point of contact who actually shows up, actually fixes things, and actually cares about the space. That's what Meridian is. A team that treats your building like it's ours, because in a way, it is.

Meridian Facility Solutions is based in Fairfield, California and begins operations in January 2027.

Leadership

Simeon Meehan, Founder

Simeon brings over a decade of hands-on experience in facility operations and commercial services across the Bay Area. He's managed teams, budgets, and vendor relationships for properties ranging from small offices to large commercial campuses, and he's seen firsthand how the right facilities partner can transform a business. At Meridian, he leads with the belief that reliability isn't a nice-to-have, it's the whole job.

One point of accountability

Many facilities juggle separate vendors for cleaning, grounds, and maintenance, each with its own contact and schedule. Meridian is designed to manage that work under one agreement. We scope the requirements, assign trained crews and vetted partners, and run quality control ourselves. Services are delivered by trained crews and vetted partners under our management and quality control, and you have one contact for scheduling, issues, and reporting.

Our values

  • Accountability. We own the result, whoever performs the task.
  • Respect. Courteous, professional conduct with every customer, visitor, and team member.
  • Reliability. Work done on the agreed schedule, to the written scope.
  • Transparency. Clear communication, honest reporting, and no surprises.
